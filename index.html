Hey, I'm Charles

NLP, CV Researcher | Deep Learning Enthusiast | IEEE Student Member

I am Charles Jeyaseelan, an emerging researcher in Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language Processing, currently completing my Integrated Master's in Computer Applications. My research interests lie at the intersection of affective computing, multimodal learning, and safe AI, where I aim to design systems that understand human communication with psychological depth and contextual accuracy.

My academic journey has involved developing deep learning models for emotion intensity estimation using texmojis, here my work was published at IEEE CCEM 2023. This experience strengthened my interest in building emotion-aware AI systems rooted in linguistic nuance and cultural sensitivity. I am currently working on transformer-based emotion modeling and V2X multimodal reinforcement learning for traffic conflict prediction using the CARLA simulator. I have also presented multiple NLP research proposals at the IEEE CCEM 2024 Pre-Conference Workshop.

My long-term vision is to advance explainable, empathetic, and human-aligned AI by bridging technical innovation with responsible deployment. I am driven by the belief that intelligent systems should not only perform well—they should understand, adapt, and respect the people they interact with.

Let's connect to explore cutting-edge ideas in NLP, deep learning, and emotionally responsible AI. Together, we can shape a future where machines understand more than just words - they understand us.

Contextual Semantic Analysis

Sreekumar NR, Narendran SM, Lasya S, Charles Jeyaseelan

IEEE CCEM Pre-Conference Workshop 2024 Presentation

Introduced a novel approach to calculate semantic similarity scores for unstructured text across 8 contextual domains. Leveraged semantic tree transformations and deep learning regressors to predict contextual scores, aiming to bridge gaps in lexicon-based methods.

Emotional Quotient: An AI-Based Approach Using Prolog

Sreekumar NR, Narendran SM, Charles Jeyaseelan

IEEE CCEM Pre-Conference Workshop 2024 Presentation

Proposed a method to estimate Emotional Quotient (EQ) using Prolog programming, focusing on six basic emotions (happy, sad, anger, fear, disgust, surprise). Designed a scoring mechanism for EQ estimation and multi-variate emotion annotation for predictive analysis.
